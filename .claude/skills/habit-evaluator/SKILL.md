# Habit Tracker Evaluator Skill

When analyzing habit tracker log data:
1. Calculate the overall completion rate for the past 7 days (Completed / Total Scheduled * 100).
2. Identify the habit with the highest streak and the habit skipped most often.
3. Provide 2 actionable coaching tips to fix skipped habits without sounding overly clinical.
